Process

How an engagement runs

  1. Map

    A discovery session maps where time goes and where Claude would earn its keep first. You get a short written plan either way.

  2. Build

    We implement the highest-value workflows first, inside your accounts, and measure them against how the work was done before.

  3. Hand over

    Documentation, training, and a playbook. Your team runs it. We stay available for the next round.

FAQ

Questions, answered plainly

Are you affiliated with Anthropic?

No. Claude Consulting is an independent practice of Designs By Dave O. We are not affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored by Anthropic. Claude is a trademark of Anthropic, PBC.

Do we need to be on a specific Claude plan?

No. Part of the first conversation is working out which plan fits, whether that is a team plan, an enterprise plan, or API access for something custom. Sometimes the answer is the plan you already have.

Is our business too small for this?

Usually not. Small teams often see the largest gains, because Claude absorbs work that would otherwise need another hire. We scope to your size.

Who owns what you build?

You do. Accounts, prompts, agents, integrations, and documentation are all yours. Nothing we build is locked to us.

Do you only work with Claude?

Claude is the focus here, but we deploy whatever fits the job. If another model or tool is the better answer for a workflow, we will say so.
